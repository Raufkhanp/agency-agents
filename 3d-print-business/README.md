# 3D Printing Business Starter Kit (India: Amazon.in / Flipkart)

This folder contains **26 free 3D models** in `models/`, grouped by product category. I chose them because they are popular and **their licences allow you to sell the printed products**.

## 1. What sells (research summary)

Nobody publishes official sales rankings for 3D‑printed items on Amazon.in or Flipkart. The categories below come up again and again in 2026 seller guides and marketplace searches. They are also cheap to print and give good margins:

| Rank | Category | Why it sells | Example in `models/` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Phone / laptop / headphone stands** | Everyone has a phone and work‑from‑home is common. Easy gift. | credit‑card phone stand, laptop stands, headphone stand |
| 2 | **Cable organizers & clips** | Cheap to make, so customers buy them in bundles | cable winder (109k downloads), cable clips |
| 3 | **Fidget toys & articulated animals** | Viral on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Popular with kids and teens. | infinity cube, spiral cone, dragon egg, articulated dragon |
| 4 | **Keychains (incl. personalised name tags)** | Small orders that sell in volume. Custom names sell at a higher price. | name keychain (editable), heart, Flexi Rex |
| 5 | **Home decor: vases, planters, moon lamps** | High perceived value for gifting (Diwali, birthdays) | moon lamp, ripple vase, rose vase, moon planter |
| 6 | **Religious / festival items** | Strong India-only demand (Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali) | Lord Ganesh idol |
| 7 | **Desk organizers / pen holders** | Bought by offices and students | remote holder, dragon pen holder |
| 8 | **Kitchen: bag clips, cookie cutters** | Useful items with repeat orders. Cookie cutters peak at Christmas. | zippy bag clip, cookie cutter |
| 9 | **Smart‑home mounts (Echo Dot etc.)** | Echo Dot owners search for exactly this, so it is easy to rank | Echo Dot corner mount |

**Tips**
- Your best sellers will be **personalised products** (name keychains, name plates, custom lithophane lamps). Mass‑market factories can't copy them.
- **Bundle** small items (e.g. "5 cable clips") so each order is worth more than the shipping cost.
- **Never sell anything with brands or characters** (Pokémon, Marvel, Mario, PlayStation logos, cricket team logos). Amazon and Flipkart remove these listings and can suspend your account. I left all such models out on purpose.

## 2. Licences — read this before selling
Every model here uses one of these licences, and **all of them allow commercial sale of prints**:
- **CC0 / Public Domain**: no conditions.
- **CC BY (Attribution)**: credit the designer, e.g. a line in your listing: *"Design by squinn, CC BY 4.0, printables.com"*.
- **CC BY‑SA**: credit the designer. If you *modify and share the file*, share it under the same licence. Selling prints is fine.

Each model folder has a `LICENSE-and-CREDIT.txt` with the designer's name, the source link and the licence.
⚠️ I skipped every "Non‑Commercial (NC)" model, including many of the most popular phone stands. Those **cannot** be sold legally.

## 4. Next steps to launch
1. **Test print** each model and pick the 5–8 with the best print time and quality.
2. **Cost each item:** filament weight (shown in your slicer) × your PLA price per kg, plus electricity, packaging and the marketplace fee. A common rule of thumb is to price at **3–4× material + packaging cost**.
3. **Register as a seller:** GSTIN for the product category (or the composition scheme), a bank account, and a brand name. Amazon.in also has *Amazon Karigar* and Flipkart has *Samarth* programs for handmade/artisan sellers. Check whether you're eligible.
4. **Photos:** use a white background for the main image, then add lifestyle shots on a desk. Show a size comparison next to a coin or hand.
5. **Start with personalisation** (name keychains, name plates, lithophanes). Then expand into seasonal items (Ganesh idols in August–September, diyas and decor before Diwali, cookie cutters before Christmas).
